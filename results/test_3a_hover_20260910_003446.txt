P4 - TRANSLATIONAL LIFT [L3 measured]
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Rotor shaft power at hover     : 68.17 W
  Rotor shaft power at 9.0 m/s   : 69.88 W
  Difference                     : +2.51% (tolerance +/-10%)
  Result                         : PASS
  Total thrust hover / cruise    : 9.932 / 10.095 N

  Shaft power measured as sum of |torque| * omega from
  getRotorStates(). A PASS means cruise costs the same
  as hover, i.e. the simulator has NO translational
  lift. RotorParams.hpp computes thrust as
  C_T * rho * n^2 * D^4, which contains no airspeed and
  no induced-velocity term, so AirSim is structurally
  incapable of reproducing DJI's 46 min flight time
  against a 40 min hover time.

  Consequence: sim-derived power can NEVER validate
  cruise endurance. That physics lives in the L2 model.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS TEST DOES NOT ESTABLISH
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The simulated airframe is not a Mavic 3. VehicleType
  SimpleFlight routes to setupFrameGenericQuad: 1.0 kg on
  0.2286 m propellers, against the Mavic 3's 0.895 kg on
  0.2388 m. Mass and rotor geometry are C++ compile-time
  constants and settings.json cannot change them.

  So no hover-endurance number here is a measurement of a
  Mavic 3. The endurance figure is the L2 model's, and the
  simulator's contribution is a trustworthy clock and an
  independently computed air density.
