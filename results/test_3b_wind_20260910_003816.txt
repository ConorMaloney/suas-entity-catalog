INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The wind penalty on energy in this simulator is far too
  small to measure. At 5 m/s the aircraft tilts about one
  degree, and thrust rises by roughly 0.017 percent. Any
  test claiming to show wind reducing range in AirSim is
  reporting an artifact of its own model, not a simulated
  effect.

  Note also what the simulated airframe is: a 1.0 kg generic
  quad, not the 0.895 kg Mavic 3. The drag identified here
  is the SIMULATOR's, which is the point - it is what makes
  the fitted-versus-source comparison a genuine test.
